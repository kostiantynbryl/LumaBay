# Game design — 0.1 Alpha

## Core loop

1. Select the next unlocked level.
2. Complete a Match-3 goal within the move limit.
3. Earn one to three stars and coins.
4. Spend stars restoring the Luma Bay lighthouse.
5. Unlock the next level and short story beat.

## Board

- 8×8 cells.
- Six piece families with distinct color and silhouette.
- No initial matches.
- At least one legal move after generation and after each resolution.
- Tap-tap and directional swipe input.

## Special pieces

- Four in a horizontal match: row light.
- Four in a vertical match: column light.
- Five pieces: aurora prism.
- T/L intersection: signal bomb.

## Alpha goals

- Collect a specified piece family.
- Clear fog layers by matching pieces on fog cells.

## Difficulty philosophy

The alpha starts forgiving and gradually increases target counts, fog coverage and tighter move limits. There are no forced purchases, ads or hidden difficulty changes.

## Accessibility

Color is never the only identifier. Every piece has a unique silhouette and a letter/symbol overlay. The interface uses large tap targets, high contrast, reduced text density and safe-area fitting.
